## Set up your tables

- Add each table with its number of seats and the area it is in (for example terrace or bar).
- Draw your floor plan so staff can see the room at a glance.
- Take a table out of use when you need to, and bring it back later.

## Manage bookings

From **Reservations** you can:

- see today's bookings and the days ahead;
- seat a party, mark the visit complete, or mark a no-show;
- add walk-ins and phone bookings yourself.

Walk-ins and bookings you add yourself never cost anything.
