Diners pay you directly. If you want to take deposits or pre-orders in the app, connect a payment account.

## Connect a payment account

1. Open **Payments** in LastTable Business.
2. Follow the steps to connect your account. Payments are processed by Stripe.
3. Once it's connected, you can turn on deposits for bookings.

## Paying LastTable

The same Payments page shows how you pay LastTable for credits and plans.
