## Invite someone

1. Open **Settings → Team**.
2. Tap **Invite** and enter their mobile number.
3. They sign in with that number and see your restaurant.

Only owners can invite people.

## Remove someone

In **Settings → Team**, choose the person and **Remove from team**. They lose access straight away.
