A **Flash Table** is an offer you put on a few tables for a short time, to fill a quiet slot.

## Create one

1. Open **Flash Tables** in LastTable Business.
2. Choose the time window, the offer and how many tables it covers.
3. Publish. Diners see it straight away.

## Good to know

- You decide the offer, the times and the number of tables.
- You can end an offer early.
- In Ireland, offers are on food: never promote alcohol.
