**Manage your tables. Fill empty seats. Pay only for new diners.**

## What you pay for

You use credits only when a diner who is **new to your restaurant** books through LastTable and comes in.

These are always free:

- returning diners and your existing customers;
- walk-ins and bookings you add yourself;
- cancellations and verified no-shows.

## Plans and credit packs

You can buy credit packs or choose a monthly plan. Current prices are on the pricing page: https://lasttable.ie/for-venues/pricing (Ireland) and https://lasttable.ae/for-venues/pricing (UAE).
