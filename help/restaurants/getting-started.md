> Official version: https://lasttable.ie/help/restaurants/getting-started

LastTable helps restaurants fill empty seats. You manage your tables in **LastTable Business**, and you pay only for genuinely new diners.

## Join

1. Open LastTable Business (the app or the website) and sign in with your mobile number.
2. Add your restaurant, or claim it if it is already listed.
3. Fill in your profile: opening hours, photos, cuisine and a short description.
4. Add your tables.
5. Turn on **Visible to diners** when the checklist is complete.

## Tips for a strong page

- A cover photo and at least four photos.
- A description of a few sentences that says what makes your place worth booking.
- Opening hours for every day you are open.
