The **Concierge** lets you ask for a table in your own words, for example *"Italian for 4 near the city centre around 8"*.

## How it works

- It only suggests restaurants and times that are free when you ask.
- It never books for you: you pick a suggestion and confirm it yourself.
- Answers are written by AI from live availability, so always check the details before you confirm.

The Concierge is not available in every country yet.
