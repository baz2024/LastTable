Some restaurants let you join their walk-in waiting line from the app, so you don't have to stand at the door.

## How it works

1. Open the restaurant's page. If it offers **Skip the Queue**, you'll see it there.
2. Choose your party size and join the line.
3. Follow your place in the line in the app, and come in when the restaurant calls you.

## Good to know

- Each restaurant decides whether it offers Skip the Queue.
- Any tip or deposit, and how it works, is shown before you join.
