Restaurant full? **Cancellation Radar** watches it for you.

## Set up a watch

1. Open the restaurant's page.
2. Tap **Watch with Radar**.
3. Choose your party size, the date and a time window.

## When a table frees up

We hold it for you for 5 minutes and let you know. Confirm it to book, or let it go.

You can stop a watch at any time from the Bookings tab.
