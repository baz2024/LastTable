> Official version: https://lasttable.ie/help/diners/flash-tables

A **Flash Table** is an offer a restaurant puts on a few tables for a short time, for example 20% off food between 18:00 and 19:00.

## How it works

- The restaurant sets the offer, the times and how many tables it covers.
- You see the offer, its conditions and how many tables are left **before** you book.
- You book it like any other table.
- The discount is applied by the restaurant when you pay your bill there.

## Good to know

- Offers end when the time window ends or the tables are gone.
- Conditions differ by restaurant: always read them on the booking screen.
