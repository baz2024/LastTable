## Change a booking

1. Open the **Bookings** tab.
2. Tap the booking.
3. Tap **Change** and pick a new time or party size.

## Cancel a booking

1. Open the **Bookings** tab.
2. Tap the booking.
3. Tap **Cancel**.

Please cancel as early as you can, so the table can go to someone else.

## If the restaurant changes your booking

Restaurants manage their own tables. If a restaurant changes or cancels your booking, you see the update in the Bookings tab.
