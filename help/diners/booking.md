> Official version: https://lasttable.ie/help/diners/booking

LastTable shows tables that are really free right now, so you can book tonight without phoning around. It is free for diners.

## Find a table

1. Open the LastTable app, or the website.
2. Search by area, cuisine or restaurant name, or browse what is free tonight.
3. Pick your party size and a time.

## Book it

1. Tap a time. The table is held for you for 5 minutes while you confirm.
2. Enter your name and mobile number.
3. Type the code we send you by text message.

That's it: you get a confirmation in the app. You pay the restaurant as usual when you dine.

## Good to know

- If you don't confirm within 5 minutes, the hold ends and the table goes back to other diners.
- Some restaurants ask for a deposit for some bookings. You see it before you confirm.
- Running late or have a question for the restaurant? Use **Call restaurant** on the restaurant's page.
