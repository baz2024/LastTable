## Signing in

LastTable verifies you with a code sent to your mobile number. You can also sign in with Apple, Google or email where they are offered; we still ask for your mobile number before your first booking.

## No code arrived?

Check the number and its country code, wait a minute and tap **Resend code**. If it still doesn't arrive, contact support.

## Your data

- **Download your data:** in the app, Profile → Privacy & your data.
- **Delete your account:** in the app, Profile → Privacy & your data → Delete account. Without the app, see the delete-your-account page on the website.
- How we use personal data is explained in the privacy notice on the website.
