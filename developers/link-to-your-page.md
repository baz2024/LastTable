> Official version: https://lasttable.ie/help

Send diners from your website, Instagram or Google profile straight to your LastTable page.

## Find your link

1. Open your restaurant's page on lasttable.ie or lasttable.ae.
2. Copy the address from your browser.

## Add a booking button to your website

```html
<a href="YOUR-LASTTABLE-PAGE-ADDRESS" rel="noopener">Book a table on LastTable</a>
```

Replace `YOUR-LASTTABLE-PAGE-ADDRESS` with the address you copied.
