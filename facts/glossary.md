> Official version: https://lasttable.ie/help

- **Flash Table:** an offer a restaurant puts on a few tables for a short time, for example 20% off food between 18:00 and 19:00.
- **Cancellation Radar:** watches a full restaurant for you and holds the next table that frees up for 5 minutes.
- **Hold:** when you tap a time, the table is kept for you for 5 minutes while you confirm.
- **Concierge:** ask for a table in your own words; it suggests only tables that are free.
- **Skip the Queue:** join a restaurant's walk-in waiting line from the app, where the restaurant offers it.
- **Credits:** what restaurants use to pay LastTable, only for genuinely new diners.
- **LastTable Business:** the app and website restaurants use to manage tables and bookings.
