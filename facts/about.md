> Official version: https://lasttable.ie/about

- **What it is:** LastTable is a real-time table booking app. It shows restaurant tables that are free right now, and lets diners book them in a few taps.
- **For diners:** free. Tables are held for 5 minutes while you confirm with a code sent to your mobile.
- **For restaurants:** LastTable Business manages tables and bookings. Restaurants pay only for genuinely new diners who book through LastTable and attend.
- **Where:** Ireland (lasttable.ie) and the United Arab Emirates (lasttable.ae).
- **Signature features:** live tables, Flash Tables, Cancellation Radar and the Concierge.
- **Website:** https://lasttable.ie
